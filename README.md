# Crisp Math Studio 🧮✨

> A local-first, offline-capable math workshop and business calculator designed for retail cashiers, students, and indie professionals. Part of the **Crisp Suite / HandleVault** ecosystem.

---

## 🌟 Features

* **Smart Tape Calculator:** Keeps a running history of your calculations like a cash register receipt so you can easily review past entries.
* **Retail & Business Quick Solvers:** Built-in tools for markup, margin, discount calculation, and cash denomination counting.
* **Night Shift & Day Shift Ergonomics:** Tailored themes designed to reduce eye strain for long-shift cashiers and night-duty workers.
* **100% Local & Private:** Runs entirely in your browser with zero server tracking or required logins. Your calculations stay on your device.
* **Lightweight & Fast:** Built with a clean, dependency-free architecture for instant loading.

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

No complex installation or build steps are required. 

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Open `index.html` in any modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari).
3. Start calculating or switch between **Night Shift** and **Day Shift** modes using the header toggle.
